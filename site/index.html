Caseward operations console

What a case handler sees during a shift: sign in, take the most urgent task, check an AI draft against the certificate, and deal with deadline alerts. These screens come from the console running against the real backend and database, filled with synthetic landlords, properties and certificates.

Worked end to end
  • Four synthetic certificates went through the real pipeline: malware scan, page rendering, text reading and the AI draft checks.
  • The flag checks caught "Satisfactory" with a C2 and a low-confidence postcode. The C1 certificate went to the top of the queue as urgent.
  • Approving a correction created the inspection and both defects, queued their deadline calculation, and opened the next task automatically.
  • The browser logged no errors.
Stand-ins in this preview
  • Certificates, landlords and people are synthetic.
  • The AI model is a stand-in. Real extraction stays off until the UK processing agreement is signed.
  • The malware scanner is a stand-in, because ClamAV isn't installed here.
  • Sign-in used a development token. The sign-in buttons for the two providers are configured placeholders.
  • Deadline rules are labelled UNVERIFIED example rules, not legal advice.
